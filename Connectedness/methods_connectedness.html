3. The total connectedness index

Averaging the off-diagonal mass gives one number for system-wide interconnectedness:

A TCI near means each series is driven mainly by its own shocks; near the system moves as one. Tracking the TCI through a rolling window dates the build-up of systemic spillovers.

Interpretation, carefully stated. These are predictive variance shares, not structural causal effects. A high means shocks to help forecast the variance of at horizon — possibly through common factors, not a direct bilateral channel. Read the network as a predictive dependence skeleton.
Generalized vs Cholesky. The generalized NET ranking is order-invariant; the Cholesky NET ranking changes with the ordering. Comparing the two gauges how robust your transmitter/receiver story is.
